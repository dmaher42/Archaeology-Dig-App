# Scarab Queen V2 Generation Prompts

Use these prompts to regenerate the full permanent Scarab Queen source set. Each output must be a production PNG sprite sheet, not placeholder art.

## Shared Style Prompt

Create a premium 2D side-view game boss sprite sheet for the Scarab Queen, a colossal Egyptian scarab guardian. She has a black stone scarab shell, turquoise enamel inlays, gold trim, Egyptian crown-like head crest, glowing teal eyes, large armored claws, segmented legs, and a raised scorpion-like tail. The style must match high-detail painted fantasy game art with warm desert lighting, crisp readable silhouette, and no cartoon simplification.

Strict sheet rules: transparent background or perfectly flat removable chroma-key background, no shadows, no UI, no text, no labels, no borders, no environment, no red circles. Every frame must be fully contained in its own cell with generous padding. No frame may overlap or include pieces of another frame. Keep the Queen the same scale and facing left in every frame. Keep a consistent ground baseline under the feet/body. The feet and body must show weight and contact with the ground.

## Sheets

### Portrait

One full-body static side-view pose of the Scarab Queen facing left. Regal, menacing, fully readable silhouette, feet grounded, tail visible, no motion blur. Single centered frame.

### Combat Idle

Eight-frame side-view idle/combat breathing sheet. Feet planted on the same ground line. Subtle shell rise, mandible movement, tail tension, and claw twitch only. No walking, no sliding, no lunge.

### Walk

Eight-frame heavy slow crawl sheet. Side view facing left. The body advances through a believable scarab gait with alternating leg contact. Feet must plant and lift visibly; the body must not appear to slide. Keep the abdomen, shell, tail, and head consistent in size. All frames aligned to the same ground baseline.

### Run / Charge

Eight-frame aggressive low charge sheet. Side view facing left. The Queen lowers her body, claws open, legs push powerfully, tail balances the movement. Strong weight transfer and clear foot contact. No frame overlap, no clipping.

### Attack Windup

Six-frame attack windup sheet. Side view facing left. The Queen braces, raises front claws, opens mandibles, and pulls back before striking. Feet remain grounded and the body mass feels heavy.

### Acid Spit

Eight-frame acid spit attack sheet. Side view facing left. The Queen plants her legs, lifts/opens head and mandibles, then spits glowing toxic green acid forward. Include acid emission only where it belongs to the attack frame; no detached random splashes outside the frame padding.

### Acid Projectile

Six-frame projectile-only sheet. Toxic green acid glob projectile traveling left, no Queen body. Keep each projectile centered in its own cell with transparent padding.

### Stagger / Hit

Five-frame hit reaction sheet. Side view facing left. The Queen recoils, shell and head jolt, legs brace, and a counter-window pose is clear. Do not change the character identity or scale.

### Death

Eight-frame death collapse sheet. Side view facing left. The Queen loses balance, drops heavily, legs fold under her, body hits the ground, and ends in a stable fallen pose. Final pose must be fully grounded and no longer walking. No body parts clipped by frame boundaries.
