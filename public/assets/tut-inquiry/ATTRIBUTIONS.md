# Cold Case: KV62 — image attributions

Real photographic assets used in the Tutankhamun investigation mode, downloaded from
Wikimedia Commons. Public-domain and CC0 images carry no attribution requirement but are
recorded here for provenance. **CC BY / CC BY-SA images must keep the credit + licence link
visible in-game** (an "Image credits" panel in the field journal is the planned home).

Originals are kept in `scratch/tut-assets/` with their source metadata in `_meta.json`.

## Held in scratch as reference (not shipped)

- `scratch/tut-assets/tut-mummified-head.jpg` (CC BY 4.0) and `kv35-younger-lady.gif`,
  `mask-in-situ-burton.jpg`, extra Burton frames `burton-2-042..045.jpg`. The mummified-head
  photo is **deliberately not used as a display asset** — per the project's museum-image
  sensitivity rule, human remains are represented by stylised scan reconstructions
  (AI-generated, labelled as reconstructions), not graphic photographs.
