# Lost Site Expedition Journey Progression Map

This document maps how the Egypt Journey should reveal story across playable sections while preserving the current room-order and game-first rules.

It supports:

- `docs/lost-site-expedition-story-bible.md`
- `docs/lost-site-expedition-story-arc.md`
- `docs/egypt-act-1-room-order.md`
- `docs/lost-site-expedition-production-bible.md`

It should guide shard, room-restoration, Anubis judgement, story-prop, and section-progression work.

---

## Core Design

The Journey should create a real player choice:

> Move quickly through the route, or restore what the site still remembers.

Quick progression should remain possible. The player can recover enough shards, survive gates, and move forward.

Deeper restoration should reveal more truth. If the player enters sacred rooms, restores fragments, and preserves evidence, Asha gains stronger story understanding and Anubis gains judgement evidence that she is different from looters.

The story should still unfold for every player, but it should unfold with different clarity:

- **Fast route:** the player understands the basic danger, but Anubis remains colder and the Queen mystery is more ambiguous.
- **Restoration route:** the player sees stronger evidence, earns more Anubis reconsideration, and reaches the Queen section with better context.

Do not create a simple trust meter. Track restored-room actions as judgement evidence.

---

## Shard Purpose

Shards should carry two related meanings without becoming a frustrating currency trap.

### Recovered Shards

Recovered shards are the total sacred fragments Asha has found.

Use recovered shard count for:

- route seal recognition
- checkpoint or route progress
- proof that Asha has explored and survived
- basic Journey pacing

Recovered shard count should not go down when Asha restores a room.

### Restored Fragments

Restored fragments are sacred pieces Asha returns, repairs, places, decodes, or uses respectfully inside story rooms.

Use restored fragments for:

- Anubis judgement evidence
- room-specific story reveals
- stronger Queen-section context
- optional revisit rewards
- later dialogue variation

Restored fragments should feel like action, not payment. Asha is not buying trust. She is proving care.

---

## Room Restoration Choice

The first three sacred rooms form a connected story chain, but remain separate interiors.

Do not directly link one chamber interior to another chamber interior.

Use this chain:

1. **Mummification Chamber: Body**
   - Player action: perform careful rites, inspect ritual objects, restore body/self fragments.
   - Story reveal: this place protects the dead as people, not as treasure containers.
   - Judgement evidence: Asha preserves rather than disturbs.
   - Next clue: a ritual/name mark points toward damaged images in the Mural Room.

2. **Mural Room: Image**
   - Player action: gather and restore broken image/scarab fragments.
   - Story reveal: the wall story has been damaged, erased, or rearranged.
   - Judgement evidence: Asha restores what others tried to erase.
   - Next clue: restored imagery exposes missing captions and scribe marks.

3. **Scribe Chamber: Name**
   - Player action: restore names, decode records, compare ritual and mural clues.
   - Story reveal: stories can corrupt memory as much as theft can damage objects.
   - Judgement evidence: Asha protects truth, not only objects.
   - Next clue: the Queen's public story is incomplete.

The chain is:

> Body -> Image -> Name

And emotionally:

> More than treasure -> damaged memory -> wounded truth

## Revisit Structure

The player should not permanently lose the story by moving forward.

Later Journey sections should create chances to return to missed sacred rooms without making the player feel forced.

Use one or more of these return methods:

- exterior shortcuts back to earlier structures
- Base Camp review map that lets Asha revisit discovered room entrances
- Anubis warning lines that point back without becoming a checklist
- restored route seals that reopen old thresholds after major section progress
- optional "before the Queen" pause where the site gives Asha one last chance to restore evidence

Revisits should require the same exterior doorway structure. They should not create direct chamber-to-chamber interior links.

---

## Anubis Pressure

Anubis should become more capable of blocking, redirecting, or testing Asha as the Journey continues, but this needs careful story logic.

Do not say Anubis simply becomes stronger because Asha travels farther.

Better framing:

- the deeper route contains older, more intact seals
- restored fragments make the protection system more responsive
- Anubis focuses more attention on Asha as she survives
- the site can push Asha back from deeper thresholds if she lacks evidence

This preserves the Story Arc rule that Anubis' power has faded while still letting later sections feel more dangerous.

Use "banishment" as soft pressure, not a harsh fail state:

- push Asha back to a recent checkpoint
- close a threshold until more evidence is restored
- trigger a short warning scene
- increase guardian presence near a refused route
- point toward a missed room or unresolved fragment

Avoid game-over punishment for choosing quick progression. The point is tension and consequence, not trapping the player.

## Section Progression Map

### 1. Desert Entry

Story purpose:

> Asha enters a protected place and realises this is more than treasure.

Primary beats:

- grounded scarab inconsistency
- Anubis rejects Asha as another trespasser
- recovered shards are introduced as fragments the seals still recognise
- sacred room restoration is introduced through Mummification, Mural, and Scribe clues

Player choice:

- move fast with recovered shards
- restore the first sacred-room fragments for deeper evidence

Anubis state:

- judgement, suspicion, and refusal
- he notices restoration, but does not trust Asha yet

Story-prop direction:

- broken ritual markers before Mummification
- scarab cuts and damaged wall traces before Mural
- name/glyph tablets before Scribe

Do not reveal:

- "It was not treasure they stole. It was memory."
- the full Queen truth
- the wider guardian network

### 2. Ruined Temple

Story purpose:

> The site judges actions, not explanations.

Primary beats:

- guardians and route seals become more deliberate
- recovered shards prove survival and observation
- restored room evidence begins to affect Anubis' lines
- missed-room hints can appear as warnings, not orders

Player choice:

- push through the temple route quickly
- return to restore an unresolved room before going deeper

Anubis state:

- still hostile
- begins to notice whether Asha restores or only passes

Story-prop direction:

- judgement thresholds
- damaged guardian markers
- seal panels that echo the first three sacred rooms

### 3. Catacombs

Story purpose:

> The damage is deeper than broken objects.

Primary beats:

- missing names, dark records, broken captions, and disturbed dead become more visible
- if the Scribe Chamber is unrestored, the player sees more confusion around names
- if restored, the player can read stronger hints about the Queen's altered memory

Player choice:

- proceed through danger with partial understanding
- return to restore names/images/body evidence before the story darkens

Anubis state:

- his certainty weakens
- he may admit the seals are failing without admitting Asha is right

Story-prop direction:

- erased names
- damaged record tablets
- repeated scribe marks
- unsettling mural fragments that feel out of order

### 4. Escape Sequence

Story purpose:

> The protection system is failing openly.

Primary beats:

- collapse, dust, unstable bridges, and guardian panic show consequence
- the site reacts differently depending on restored evidence
- Anubis can banish, block, or push Asha back from deeper thresholds if she lacks enough judgement evidence

Player choice:

- survive and leave with limited truth
- accept a final return opportunity before the Queen section or discovery entrance

Anubis state:

- not friendly, but less certain
- if Asha restored rooms, he recognises action
- if she rushed, he still sees an intruder who survived

Story-prop direction:

- cracked seals
- falling memory panels
- broken routes where restoration evidence can calm or clarify the environment

### 5. Dig Site Entrance

Story purpose:

> Survival turns into interpretation.

Primary beats:

- the Journey hands back to archaeology
- Asha reviews evidence rather than simply claiming victory
- Base Camp can surface missed-room leads
- the Queen mystery becomes the next focused question

Player choice:

- continue with the evidence already found
- revisit discovered rooms before committing to the next major truth reveal

Anubis state:

- he may allow access, but that is not the same as trust
- restored evidence can unlock colder-but-respectful lines

Story-prop direction:

- field notes, mapped fragments, sealed record markers
- evidence displays that reflect restored or missed rooms

---

## Mandatory Story Spine

Even if the player rushes, these truths must still be visible:

1. Asha is not trusted.
2. The site is protected, not abandoned.
3. Shards are sacred fragments the seals recognise.
4. The first sacred rooms suggest this is more than treasure.
5. The Queen's story is contested.
6. Anubis judges action more than speech.
7. The Journey leads into evidence interpretation, not simple victory.

Restoration should deepen these truths, not replace the main path.

---

## Implementation Direction

When this design is implemented, use the existing canonical systems:

- `RELIC_SHARDS` / `relicShardCount` for recovered shard progress
- `SECRET_COLLECTIBLES` and room flags for restored room fragments
- `STORY_PROPS` for exterior story breadcrumbs and chamber approach props
- existing Journey scene IDs for Mummification, Mural, and Scribe interiors
- existing route gates and checkpoints for section progression

Do not create:

- a second shard system
- a direct chamber-to-chamber room link
- a visible trust meter
- a separate story progression framework
- a hard fail state for missing optional room restoration

The safest implementation path is to add small room-specific restoration flags and Anubis dialogue variations first, then tune route gates and revisit points after the story flow is playable.
